Finishing a course does not show what a learner can actually do. Maraqi al-Nuha is built on a different record: an evidence ledger, where what counts is the work a learner produces and what it demonstrates, not attendance or completed modules. I wanted that idea to be clear from the first page, which is why the project starts as a public site and a curriculum before it becomes an application.

The public site is static HTML and JavaScript, written in both Arabic and English with right-to-left layout for Arabic, separate curriculum pages for each language, and bilingual SEO and Open Graph cards so links share properly in either language. The platform repository holds the domain model behind it, organised around evidence of skills rather than around classes and grades, which is what keeps it from becoming yet another school management system.
