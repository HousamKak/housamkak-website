- **Wordle (English and Arabic):** a browser Wordle clone with a separate Arabic version.
- **Maze game:** a browser maze game with its own soundtrack.
- **Crosswords puzzle:** a crossword builder that lays out a grid from a clue set, with 2D and 3D graph views of how the words link.
- **Dama:** a browser version of dama, the draughts game.
- **CountDown widget:** a large, always-visible desktop countdown for timeboxing work.
- **OBS round mask:** a C++ OBS Studio plugin that masks a source into a circle, for camera bubbles.
- **TranslatePDF bot:** a Telegram bot that translates PDF documents.
- **Geometry Explorer:** an interactive web app for exploring geometric constructions.
- **ASCII art:** a proof of concept that turns images into ASCII art.
- **Hand keyboard:** a generative model of typing motion, turning text into two-hand joint trajectories and simulated finger-worn sensor signals.
- **Fridge diagnostics:** a small tool for diagnosing refrigerator door faults.
- **DepeNsee:** a dependency visualiser for JavaScript and TypeScript projects.
- **WhereTime:** time tracking with visual breakdowns of where the day went.
- **Wara2:** a Telegram bot for playing the card game Li5a with AI players.
